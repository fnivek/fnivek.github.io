AURA JUMP
Made by Bryce and Landon Holcomb
Starring Aura the bearded dragon

Help Aura bounce up the rocks, catch crickets, and splat the angry veggies!

PLAY HERE: https://fnivek.github.io/aura-jump/


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
- Aura jumps by herself. You steer her.
- iPad or phone: hold the LEFT side of the screen to go left, the RIGHT side to go right.
- Computer: arrow keys, or A and D.
- Catch crickets. Each one is 1 ammo (you start with 3).
- Tap the big orange SPIT button (or press Up, W or Space on a computer) to spit a cricket
  straight up. Hit a veggie to splat it for 100 points.
- Land on top of a veggie to squash it. But if you bump into one from the side or below, it gets Aura!
- Green leaves give a super jump. Blue rocks slide. Cracked rocks break when you land on them.
- Do not fall off the bottom!
- The button next to SPIT turns the sound on and off.


PLAY ON AN IPAD OR IPHONE (the easy way)
----------------------------------------
1. Open https://fnivek.github.io/aura-jump/ in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow pointing up).
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
4. Aura Jump is now an app on your Home Screen with Aura's face on it. Tap it to play full screen.

No sound? Turn the volume up, check that the button next to SPIT says "Sound: on", then tap the game once.


PLAY ON A COMPUTER WITHOUT INTERNET
-----------------------------------
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser and works without the internet.

HOW IT IS MADE
--------------
The whole game is one file: game.js. It is written in JavaScript, the language every web browser speaks.
Open it in any text editor and read it from top to bottom: Setup, Sounds and music, The game world,
Controls, Update (the rules), Draw (the pictures), and The game loop.
Try changing JUMP, GRAVITY or the melody at the top, save, and reload the game to see what happens!
